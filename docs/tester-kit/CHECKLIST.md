# Your first test: about 10 minutes

[Setup help](README.md) · [Send feedback](FEEDBACK.md)

Try this when you are **not live or recording**. Skip anything you do not use or cannot find. You do not need to fix a problem yourself.

For each step, note **Worked**, **Problem**, or **Skipped**. These boxes are a checklist to copy or print, not a form that sends results to John.

- [ ] **Open the app.** Use the Desktop or Start Menu shortcut. Can you find the version near the app name? Write it down.
- [ ] **Connect OBS.** Follow the [setup guide](README.md). Does the Handler recognize OBS?
- [ ] **Read Preflight.** This is the before-stream checklist. Is it clear what is ready, what needs attention, and what you chose not to use? An unconnected service may show a warning; that alone is not a failure.
- [ ] **Try the look.** Open **Appearance**. Try Broadcast Console and Vibe Dashboard. If Tactical Console is available, try it too. Change the color theme. Are the text and buttons easy to read?
- [ ] **Try both modes.** Switch between Simple and Advanced. Which one would you use? In Advanced, use **Add to Simple** on a panel you want, then return to Simple and look for it.
- [ ] **Try the guided demo.** In Advanced, find **Stream Health guided demo**. Choose **Start guided demo** and follow its buttons. Only this panel uses sample data; the rest of the dashboard still controls real connections. Do not press broadcast controls elsewhere.
- [ ] **Optional: change a scene.** With OBS still offline and not recording, note its current scene, choose another scene in the Handler, and check OBS. Return to the original scene afterward. Skip this if you do not want to change anything in OBS.
- [ ] **Check what stays saved.** Choose **Exit demo** if it is running. Close the browser tab, then reopen John's Twitch Handler from its shortcut. Did your appearance choice and OBS connection stay saved?
- [ ] **Send a quick reply.** What confused you, what did not work, and what would make you use it regularly? “Everything worked” is helpful too.

## Save these for a later test

There is no need to go live, record, run an ad, send a chat message, raid anyone, change OBS quality settings, or connect extra services for the first test.

When you and John agree to test during a planned stream, you can also check highlight markers, Replay Buffer saves, Twitch clips, and the After stream report. Replay saves need the Replay Buffer enabled and running in OBS. Stream reports need an actual tracked session; the guided demo does not create one. Clips, messages, ads, and raids can affect your real channel, so leave those alone until you intend to use them.
